State: Tamil Nadu

Capital: Chennai

About the state:

A center for Dravidian culture, it is famous for its monumental ancient temples and classical Bharatanatyam dance. In 2025, it celebrated the 1,000-year anniversary of Rajendra Chola I's maritime expedition.

Noticeable Tourist Places: Meenakshi Amman Temple (Madurai), Mahabalipuram, Ooty, and Kanyakumari.
